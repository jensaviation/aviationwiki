Transparent references

Where the aircraft facts come from.

Specifications can vary by model, engine, configuration, and operating rule. The wiki prioritizes official manufacturer material and clearly labels approximate or family-level values.

Editorial method

1. Primary first

Manufacturer manuals, government fact sheets, type certificates, and flight manuals override secondary records.

2. Exact matches only

Imported data requires an exact manufacturer and model match. Family-page redirects are rejected for variant records.

3. Missing stays hidden

Unconfirmed fields are omitted rather than estimated. Figures remain general reference—not operational data.
